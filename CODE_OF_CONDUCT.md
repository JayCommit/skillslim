# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a GitHub issue: https://github.com/JayCommit/skillslim/issues

Maintainers will review reports and may warn, restrict, or remove contributions or contributors.
